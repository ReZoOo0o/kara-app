Kara

Kara is a personal home assistant that its owner runs on a computer in their own home. It is not a product and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Who uses it: only the owner. There are no other users and no accounts.

What it accesses: with the owner's permission, Kara reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar events and Google Tasks, so it can plan the owner's day and send reminders to the owner.

Where the data goes: the data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, published or given to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

How long it is kept: only as long as the owner keeps using Kara. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which Kara can no longer read or write anything.

Contact: the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
